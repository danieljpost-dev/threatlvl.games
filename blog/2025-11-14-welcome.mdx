# Welcome to ThreatLVL Games!

We're excited to introduce ThreatLVL Games, a comprehensive suite of online tools designed specifically for running and playing 5e-compatible tabletop RPG games.

{/* truncate */}

## Our Mission

Running a great tabletop RPG game requires juggling multiple tasks: tracking initiative, managing character sheets, looking up spell details, calculating encounter difficulty, and rolling dice. Game masters and players deserve tools that make these tasks effortless, not burdensome.

That's why we built ThreatLVL Games.

## What We're Launching With

Today, we're launching with five core tools:

### 🎲 Dice Roller
Our advanced dice rolling engine supports everything from simple d20 rolls to complex expressions with advantage, disadvantage, and custom modifiers. Save your favorite rolls and access them with a single click.

### ⚔️ Character Builder
Create and manage 5e-compatible characters with our intuitive character builder. Level up, track spell slots, manage inventory, and export your character data in multiple formats.

### 👥 Initiative Tracker
Keep combat flowing smoothly with our real-time initiative tracker. Track HP, apply conditions, manage legendary actions, and never lose track of whose turn it is.

### ⚡ Spell Tracker
Browse the complete SRD spell list, track prepared spells, manage spell slots, and quickly reference spell details during gameplay.

### 🐉 Encounter Builder
Design balanced encounters using official CR guidelines. Our encounter builder automatically calculates difficulty and helps you create memorable combat scenarios.

## Built for the Community

ThreatLVL Games is built by game masters for game masters. We understand the challenges of running games online because we face them ourselves. Every feature has been designed with real gaming sessions in mind.

### Open Integration

We believe tools should work together, not in isolation. That's why we're launching with:

- **REST API**: Integrate with your own tools and workflows
- **VTT Support**: Works alongside Roll20, Foundry, and other platforms
- **Discord Bot**: Bring game tools directly into your Discord server
- **Export Options**: Your data, your format, your control

### Commitment to the SRD

ThreatLVL Games is built on the System Reference Document (SRD) under the Open Game License. We respect Wizards of the Coast's intellectual property while providing powerful tools for the community.

## What's Next?

This is just the beginning. We have big plans for ThreatLVL Games:

- **Campaign Management**: Track multiple campaigns, organize sessions, and share resources
- **World Building Tools**: Create maps, manage NPCs, and build your setting
- **Mobile Apps**: Native apps for iOS and Android
- **Homebrew Support**: Create and share custom content
- **Automation Features**: Let AI help with game prep and rule lookups
